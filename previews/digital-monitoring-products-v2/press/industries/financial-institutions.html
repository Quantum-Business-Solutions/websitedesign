Questions from financial institutions

Does DMP have anything for banks?+

Yes. DMP lists financial institutions as an industry, publishes a financial institutions brochure and makes the TMSentry panel for teller machines.

Does DMP sell directly to building owners?+

DMP sells through authorized dealers and distributors. A building owner starts with a dealer, and DMP's inside sales team helps with system design.

What does DMP make?+

Intrusion, access control, fire and video systems, with the communicators, keypads, wireless devices, Virtual Keypad and software that go with them.

How do I reach a person?+

Inside Sales is 877-757-4367, Technical Support 888-436-7832 and Customer Service 866-266-2826. The contact page lists hours and email addresses.

Where do I find datasheets and installation guides?+

In the technical documentation library on dmp.com, which holds spec sheets, installation and programming guides, quick start guides and technical updates.
