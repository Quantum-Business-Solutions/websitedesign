## The design read

Reading this as: **a B2B network-equipment vendor site** for **enterprise, campus, hospital, school and government IT leaders, plus telco operators**, with a **calm, precise, engineered-confidence** language: proof and clarity over hype.

## Audience

IT directors, CIOs and facilities leads in hospitals, schools, hotels, airports and government, plus telco network engineers. They need proof of reliability and security, cost savings, and plain explanation of what Optical LAN is. They distrust jargon walls and vendor fluff.
