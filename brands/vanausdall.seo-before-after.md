# SEO and AEO: where vanausdall.com stood, and where the rebuild stands

Both sites were scored on the same 17 checks: title, city in title, meta description, one H1, H2 structure, question headings, word count and depth, FAQ markup, Service or Article markup, LocalBusiness markup, review markup, image alt text, internal links, canonical, share image and a form or phone number. The old site was crawled on 8 September 2026 (171 pages). The rebuild was scored live on 29 October 2026 (152 pages, with seven retired pages redirected to their nearest live page).

## The headline

The average page score went from **53** to **96** out of 100. On the old site, 2 pages were an A and 100 were a D or an F. On the rebuild, every page is an A.

## Also checked beyond the score

- **Structured data.** Every block parses. Organization, LocalBusiness, WebSite, FAQPage, Service, BreadcrumbList, Article, BlogPosting and Review are present where they belong, with no repeated declarations. Two gaps remain and both need VAF: the Evansville street address, and publish dates for the 18 case studies.
- **Blog.** 335 posts keep their original publish dates, each with a summary, FAQ markup, BlogPosting and breadcrumb markup, and links to the matching service pages. HubSpot's own duplicate blog markup is switched off so there is one correct block per post.
- **Redirects.** 218 rows cover every old page, seven retired pages (COVID-19, temperature kiosks, survey, TW Telecom) and 47 blog URLs that lose a double hyphen.
- **Figures.** 342 sentences with figures were checked against the old site: 332 match what VAF already published, 10 contain a number VAF has to confirm. The Net Promoter Score was corrected to the audited 92.8. The claim "Indiana's largest" was reworded to "one of Indiana's largest independently owned" because only that version has an outside source.
