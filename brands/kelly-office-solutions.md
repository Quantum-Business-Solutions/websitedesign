## Guardrails specific to this client

- Never put green text on white without the derived ink; never put green on navy without checking
  (`#65BC7B` on `#000545` is fine at ~7:1, but the *bright* `#59BD3B` is the one in the logo — pick
  one and stop)
- The customer-facing utility links (portal, service, meter reads, supplies) never move below the
  fold on mobile; they are why most visitors come
- Do not drop Pure Technology or Mailing Solutions to make the nav tidy — both earn real search
  terms and both are revenue
- "Kelly Services" (the staffing firm) is a real confusion in search. The Organization schema,
  `sameAs`, and the H1 all exist partly to tell engines which Kelly this is
