About Quantum FAQ

Who is Quantum Business Solutions?

Quantum Business Solutions is a revenue operations agency founded by Shawn Peterson. With 8+ years of experience and 500+ businesses transformed, we specialize in building scalable growth engines using HubSpot, ZoomInfo, ConnectAndSell, and AI-powered automation.

What industries does Quantum serve?

We primarily serve B2B companies in office technology, managed services, SaaS, and professional services. Our systems are built for companies with 10–500 employees that want to scale revenue without scaling headcount.

Where is Quantum Business Solutions located?

We're a US-based company that works with clients nationwide. Our team operates remotely, which allows us to serve clients across all time zones with dedicated support and regular strategy calls.

We're the only growth agency that HubSpot, ZoomInfo, and ConnectAndSell all hand-pick to deliver results for their clients. We measure success by your P&L impact — not hours billed or tools deployed.
