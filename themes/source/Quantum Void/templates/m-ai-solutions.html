— Implementation The FOCUS Framework Don't start with the technology

Start with the problem. A five-step process for identifying, implementing, and scaling AI. F Find the Friction Map workflows and identify tasks that are repetitive, time-consuming, error-prone, or soul-crushing. O Organize Your Data Audit data quality. Are CRM records complete? Documents organized? Knowledge base current? Fix the foundation first. C Choose Your First Win Pick one use case that's high-impact, low-risk, and measurable. A single success builds momentum for everything else. U Unleash and Iterate Deploy with human-in-the-loop. AI generates, human approves. Measure everything. Refine prompts and workflows. S Scale What Works Proven ROI on one use case? Expand systematically. Document, train the team, apply to the next opportunity.

— Avoid These Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them ️ Mistake 1: Starting with Technology, Not Problems ‘We need AI' is not a strategy

‘We need to reduce lead response time from 4 hours to 4 minutes' is a strategy. Start with the problem. ️ Mistake 2: Ignoring Data Quality AI amplifies whatever it's given. If your CRM data is 60% accurate, your AI outputs will be 60% useful at best. Clean data is the prerequisite. ️ Mistake 3: Going All-In Instead of Piloting Don't sign an annual contract your team hasn't tested. Run a 30-day pilot with 3–5 users. Measure specific outcomes. Then decide. ️ Mistake 4: Expecting AI Without Human Oversight Human-in-the-loop — where AI drafts and humans approve — isn't a temporary crutch. It's the permanent best practice. ️ Mistake 5: Underinvesting in Training The biggest AI failures aren't technology failures — they're adoption failures. Budget training time, not just software cost. ️ Mistake 6: Treating AI Like a One-Time Project AI implementation is a capability, not a project. The best results come from continuous iteration month over month.
