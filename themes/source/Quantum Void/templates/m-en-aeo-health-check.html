How the Analysis Works How does the AEO Health Check analyze your site?

The audit scores your site across five dimensions: AEO Infrastructure (30%), Content Quality Signals (20%), SEO Authority (25%), AI Engine Citations (25%), and a composite letter grade. Every domain runs through the same five-dimension scoring rubric. Each dimension produces a 0-100 score and contributes a weighted slice to your composite grade. Dim 1 AEO Infrastructure 30% Weight Does your site have llms.txt? Sitemap? Robots configured to allow AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot)? Does your homepage have Organization schema? Person schema for the founder? FAQPage schema? Service schema? Meta description in the right length range? Why it matters: AI engines need machine-readable signals to confidently cite you. Missing schema is the most common reason a real expert gets ignored. Dim 2 Content Quality Signals 20% Weight Visible author bylines on content. Publish and updated dates. Citations to authoritative external sources (Gartner, HubSpot, Forrester, academic). Proper H1 and H2 structure. The Google E-E-A-T signals that AI engines now weight heavily. Why it matters: AI engines verify expertise the same way humans do — by checking whether a real, named person stands behind the claim. Dim 3 SEO Authority 25% Weight · SEMrush Data Your domain authority rank (US database). Total organic keywords ranking. Estimated monthly organic traffic. 3-month traffic trend (up, flat, declining). Top 10 keywords by traffic share with current positions. Why it matters: Traditional SEO authority remains a strong proxy for 'is this site trustworthy enough to cite' — even for AI engines that don't directly rely on Google rankings. Dim 4 AI Engine Citations 25% Weight · Live API Queries We send three generic questions about your company and space to Perplexity, OpenAI, and Anthropic — nine live API queries total. Then we check whether your domain or company name shows up in their responses. This is the closest thing to a real test of 'is my company invisible in AI search?' Why it matters: Every other AEO tool gives you theory. This dimension tells you what AI engines actually say about you today. Out Composite Grade and Prioritized Recommendations The Output All four dimensions roll into a single A-through-F composite grade. The report then identifies your top 7 recommendations in priority order — Critical first, then High, Medium, and a Next Step. Each recommendation explains the gap, why it matters, and what to do about it. You get: Branded HTML email report · Letter grade · Sub-scores · Top recommendations

How Scoring Works How is your AEO score calculated?

Your composite grade is calculated as: Infrastructure (30%) + Content Quality (20%) + SEO Authority (25%) + AI Citations (25%). Letter grades: A 90+, B 70-89, C 50-69, D 30-49, F under 30. Scores aren't guesses. Every dimension uses a transparent rubric we've calibrated from auditing 200+ B2B sites. Here's how the scoring works on the two dimensions most teams ignore. Infrastructure (100 pts max) Infrastructure Sub-Score Breakdown llms.txt present: 25 pts Sitemap.xml present: 10 pts Organization schema: 15 pts Person schema: 10 pts FAQPage schema: 10 pts AI crawlers allowed: 10 pts WebSite schema: 5 pts Service schema: 5 pts Robots.txt present: 5 pts Meta description optimal: 5 pts AI Citations (100 pts max) Citation Rate Scoring Citation rate × 100 = your score Each engine (Perplexity, OpenAI, Anthropic) gets three queries. If your domain or company name appears in 6 of 9 responses, your citation rate is 67% and your sub-score is 67/100. The most common starting state for B2B sites that haven't done AEO work: 0/9 citations. The threshold for a passing grade in this dimension: 4/9 citations. Composite Grade Formula Composite = Infrastructure (30%) + Content Quality (20%) + SEO Authority (25%) + AI Citations (25%). Letter grades: A 90+, B+ 80-89, B 70-79, C+ 60-69, C 50-59, D+/D 30-49, F <30.

Honest Limits What does the AEO Health Check NOT cover?

The audit does not cover: deep content quality review, guarantee of citation, full SEO audit, every AI engine (Gemini and others), full-site crawl, or custom strategy. The audit is genuinely useful but has clear limits. Honest scoping of what it doesn't cover. × A complete content audit This evaluates infrastructure and signals. It doesn't evaluate the actual quality of your blog content, messaging, or thought leadership. A complete content audit is a different engagement. × A guarantee of citation Fixing the recommendations dramatically improves the odds, but no AEO tactic guarantees AI engines will cite you. Authority compounds over time and requires sustained content investment. × A full SEO audit We sample SEMrush data and look at domain authority, but a real SEO audit involves keyword gap analysis, backlink profile review, and technical SEO that's out of scope here. × A test of every AI engine We query Perplexity, OpenAI, and Anthropic — the three engines that matter most for B2B research today. Google Gemini, ChatGPT search, and others may report different results. × An on-page SEO crawl We only crawl your homepage to evaluate schemas and content signals. We don't crawl your full site or run technical SEO checks on internal pages. × A custom strategy The recommendations are prioritized but generalized. A custom AEO strategy for your specific business takes 30-60 minutes of human review — that's what the free strategy call is for.
