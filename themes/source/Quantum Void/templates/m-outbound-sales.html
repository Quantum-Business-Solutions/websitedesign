Outbound Sales FAQ What is outbound sales and how does it differ from inbound?

Outbound sales is proactive prospecting — your team reaches out to potential customers through calls, emails, and social selling rather than waiting for leads to come to you. How many calls should sales reps make per day? With traditional dialing, a good rep makes 50–80 calls per day. With ConnectAndSell, reps can have 8–12 live conversations per hour. What's a good connect rate for outbound sales? Industry average is 3–5%. Using ConnectAndSell and ZoomInfo together, our clients achieve 8–15% connect rates. Do I need to hire more sales reps to scale outbound? Not necessarily. Our framework helps you get more from your existing team first — most clients double pipeline before needing to add headcount. How quickly can I start seeing pipeline from outbound? Most clients see new meetings within the first 2 weeks. Meaningful pipeline impact typically shows within 60–90 days.
