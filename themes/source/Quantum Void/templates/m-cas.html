ConnectAndSell FAQ What is ConnectAndSell and how does it work?

ConnectAndSell is a sales acceleration platform that gives reps live, connected conversations on demand. Their patented technology uses real human agents to dial through your prospect lists in parallel, screen out gatekeepers and voicemails, and patch your reps directly into live conversations — 8 to 12 per hour instead of the 1 or 2 per day most reps average with traditional dialing. How does ConnectAndSell compare to traditional auto-dialers? Traditional auto-dialers still leave your reps stuck navigating phone trees, leaving voicemails, and waiting for ring-no-answers. ConnectAndSell handles all of that off-platform with live agents, so your rep only gets on the phone when there's a real human ready to talk. The result is roughly 10x more live conversations per hour without burning out your team. Who is ConnectAndSell a good fit for? B2B sales teams running outbound — particularly SDR/BDR teams with at least 3-5 reps making calls to mid-market or enterprise contacts. It's most valuable when your average deal size justifies the per-conversation cost, your TAM is large enough to fuel high call volume, and you need to compress the time between dial and live conversation. How does Quantum implement ConnectAndSell with HubSpot and ZoomInfo? We integrate ConnectAndSell with your HubSpot CRM and ZoomInfo data so prospect lists flow automatically into the dialer, every conversation logs back to the contact record, and call dispositions trigger HubSpot workflows for follow-up. This eliminates manual list-building and ensures no live conversation falls through the cracks. What kind of results should I expect from ConnectAndSell? Most QBS clients see 8-15% live connect rates (vs. the 3-5% industry average for traditional dialing), 5-10x more meetings booked per rep per week, and meaningful pipeline impact within the first 60-90 days. Specific outcomes depend on list quality, talk track strength, and rep coachability — all areas we help you optimize alongside the technology.
