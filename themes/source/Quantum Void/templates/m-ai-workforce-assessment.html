FAQ Common questions about the 8-week program How is this different from the free AI Assessment?

The free 2-session assessment produces a Readiness Scorecard, Opportunity Map, and 30/60/90 Roadmap — enough for teams who want a directional plan they'll execute themselves. The 8-week paid program goes much deeper: we quantify the financial impact employee by employee, build the implementation plan together with your team, and stay engaged for the full 8 weeks as you ship the first wave of AI workflows. Do I need to take the free assessment first? Not strictly required, but strongly recommended. The free assessment is our standard qualifier and helps both sides confirm fit before committing to the paid program. Most clients who book the 8-week program have gone through the free version first. What if we don't have a HubSpot Pro subscription? The framework applies to any CRM environment — Salesforce, HubSpot Starter, Pipedrive, even Excel-driven teams. We are a HubSpot Diamond Partner, so HubSpot-native customers get the deepest integration recommendations. We'll confirm CRM compatibility before sending an SOW. Who needs to attend the weekly sessions? Your internal champion (typically RevOps lead, Director of Operations, or VP) must attend every session. Beyond that, depends on the week — Week 1 ideally includes 2-3 senior stakeholders; build weeks include the team members who'll own the workflows; Week 8 includes the executive sponsor. What's the time commitment from our side? Weekly 60-90 minute working sessions, plus 2-4 hours of internal work between sessions (reviewing materials, gathering data, championing internal alignment). Less in early weeks, more during build weeks. We design the engagement to fit a busy operator's calendar. Can we pause or extend the engagement? One pause of up to 2 weeks is allowed without penalty (illness, leadership transition, unexpected event). Extensions beyond 8 weeks are charged at hourly rates. We've designed the program to fit 8 weeks reliably; pauses are the exception not the norm. What about data privacy and AI governance? Every Quantum AI build uses enterprise-grade APIs (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini) configured with Zero Data Retention by default — your prompts and data are never used to train models. We follow ISO 42001:2023 AI Governance best practices and design every system with audit logs, PII redaction, and role-based access from the start. What happens if we want to engage further after Week 8? Optional. Three paths: take everything and self-run; fixed-fee builds for additional use cases ($5K–$25K each); or monthly retainer for ongoing partnership ($1K–$5K/month).
